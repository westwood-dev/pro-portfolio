### Join the Hanger beta

Hanger is an iOS app for managing your wardrobe and building outfits. You've been invited to try it out before it launches — thanks for helping test.

<Spacer />

#### What you'll need

- An iPhone running **iOS 17** or later
- The **TestFlight** app installed from the App Store

<Spacer />

#### How to install

**1. Get TestFlight**

If you don't have it already, <a href="https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/testflight/id899247664" target="_blank">download TestFlight from the App Store</a>. It's Apple's official beta testing platform. It's free, safe, and takes about 30 seconds.

**2. Accept the beta invite**

Open the link below on your iPhone:

<a href="https://testflight.apple.com/join/tZJ98cqY" target="_blank" style={{ display: 'inline-block', marginTop: '0.5rem' }}>**Join the Hanger beta**</a>

Tap **Accept** on the invite screen, then **Install**.

**3. Open Hanger**

Once installed, open Hanger from your home screen and create an account. That's it.

<Spacer />

#### What to try

- Add a few items from your wardrobe — photograph them directly or share images from Photos
- Build an outfit on the canvas
- Try the outfit suggestions (set your location when prompted so weather works)

<Spacer />

#### Sending feedback

If something breaks or feels off, shake your phone inside the app to send a report — it attaches a screenshot and device info automatically. Or just message me directly.

Beta builds update automatically through TestFlight, so you'll always have the latest version without needing to reinstall.
