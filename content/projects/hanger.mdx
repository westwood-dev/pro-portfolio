## iOS wardrobe management and outfit builder

*This project is in early development*

![](/images/hanger/hanger-hero.png)


Hanger is an iOS app that helps you organise your wardrobe and build outfits. Add clothing items by photographing them — the app automatically removes the background and categorises each piece. From there, arrange items on a canvas to plan outfits, get weather-aware suggestions, and track what you actually wear.

<Spacer />

#### Features

- **Wardrobe management** — photograph items, auto-remove backgrounds, tag by category and season
- **Outfit canvas** — drag and drop items to compose and save outfits
- **Smart suggestions** — weather and season-aware outfit recommendations
- **Share to add** — share images directly from Photos or Safari into the app

#### Tech stack

- React Native / Expo (iOS)
- Supabase (auth, database, storage)
- TensorFlow Lite for on-device ML (background removal, clothing classification)
- MMKV for local persistence with per-user isolation
- Offline queue for wardrobe edits without connectivity
