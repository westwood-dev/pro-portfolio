## A Blender study in fire and light

A self-directed render exploring fire and explosion simulation in Blender, and the composition and lighting opportunities they open up. An abandoned American-style motel, an empty pool, and a car very much on fire.

![Pool Fire](/images/pool-fire/cover.webp)

Part of an ongoing series of scenes — I plan to keep pushing the same lighting-led approach across new setups.
