## Rendered spaces, and the feeling of being watched

This started as a university brief about three shapes — circle, square, triangle — and became a project I keep returning to. Each render places a single plastic garden chair into a space, real or imagined, so the viewer becomes an observer of somewhere that quietly implies loneliness. The chair is the constant; the emptiness around it does the work.

The pieces are built to feel like looking through a window rather than watching through a screen — a quieter, more uncomfortable kind of observation. New scenes get added whenever one surfaces.
