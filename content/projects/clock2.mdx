## A digital clock built from analog clocks

The brief was open: make a clock, any way you like, using p5.js. So I made one out of other clocks. 168 analog faces sit in a grid, and their hands rotate into position to draw the digits of the current time — including the dividing colon — like a segmented display made of moving parts.

![Clock²](/images/clock2/cover.png)

It's a small idea executed precisely: the whole thing is driven by mapping each digit to hand angles and letting the faces animate into place every second.
